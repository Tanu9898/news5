Jatt Di Queen: Meet the Cast and Creative Team Behind the Upcoming Punjabi Movie Releasing in 2027

The upcoming Punjabi movie Jatt Di Queen is attracting attention following the release of its first-look poster. Presented by Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions, the film is gearing up for a theatrical release on 12 February 2027.

The project brings together a sizeable cast and an experienced technical team, with Arun Kumar Barnwal directing the film and Sumit Bhatt credited as the writer.

King B Chouhan Leads the Cast of Jatt Di Queen

The cast of Jatt Di Queen includes King B Chouhan, Rumman Ahmad, Malwai Partap, Sammy Gill, Honey Mattu, Neha Dayal, Pahulpreet Singh, Lucky Kalsi, Deush Mehra and Sukhdev Barnala.

The ensemble cast is set to bring together different performers for what the makers are presenting as a new addition to Punjabi cinema.

The film is produced by Prabh Goraya and Vicky Kamboj, under the banner of Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions.

Arun Kumar Barnwal Directs Jatt Di Queen

Arun Kumar Barnwal is directing Jatt Di Queen, while Sumit Bhatt has written the film. Ajay Maurya is the Executive Producer.

Behind the camera, cinematography is being handled by Harnek Singh and Rah Sharma. Their work will play an important role in establishing the visual identity of the upcoming Punjabi film.

Rohit Dhiman is the Editor, while Harshdeep Singh is credited as Art Director.

Full Technical Team of the Punjabi Film

The production team includes several professionals working across different departments. Jasdip Singh is the Assistant Director, while Vishal serves as Camera Operator.

Jaskaran Chogawan is credited as DIT, with Sharandeep Singh serving as Gaffer Assistant. Manpreet Singh is the Focus Puller and Kuldeep Singh is the Gaffer.

The title song has been edited by Gurjeet Singh, with choreography by Monika Rani. Deush Mehra is also credited as Choreographer.

Jatt Di Queen Music Team

Music is an important component of the project, with Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy serving as Music Directors.

The lyricists associated with the film include Happy Raikoti, Youngveer, Amber Vashisht, Abeer and Sufraaz.

The singing lineup features Malwai Partap, along with Manoj Gadarpuriya and Sachin Dahar.

Production and Digital Partners

Post-production for Jatt Di Queen is being handled by Rabab Studio, while AME Digital has been announced as the Digital Partner.

The promotional artwork has also brought together K Raj Famous Films, credited for Poster Stills, and Manoj Zore, who is responsible for Poster Design.

Jatt Di Queen Release Date

Following the unveiling of its first-look poster, Jatt Di Queen has officially entered the audience's attention ahead of its theatrical release.

The film is scheduled to release in cinemas on 12 February 2027, allowing audiences to celebrate Valentine's Day with a new Punjabi movie.
