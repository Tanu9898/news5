Kamz Inkzone and His Punjabi Music Journey: From Speed Records to Working With Major Artists

Kamz Inkzone, the artist name of Kamal Jot Singh, has been associated with several different aspects of the Punjabi music industry, from music production and songwriting to artist collaborations, video production and DJ music.

His professional journey began in 2017, but it was his experience with Speed Records and subsequent collaborations that helped him develop a broader understanding of the music business.

The Beginning With “Ink You”

Kamal Jot Singh started his music journey in 2017 under the name Kamz Inkzone. His first track was “Ink You,”which marked the beginning of his journey as a music artist.

During his early career, he also invested in a big-budget music video in Dubai. This gave him early exposure to the international production environment and helped him understand the importance of visual storytelling in music.

Learning the Music Business at Speed Records

Kamz Inkzone later worked with Speed Records, where he gained practical experience across several aspects of music production.

His work exposed him to music editing, lyrics, video production and the overall process of creating and releasing music. Rather than focusing on just one role, he learned how different departments contribute to a finished song and music video.

This experience eventually helped him become more independent as a creator.

2019: Befikra With Ninja

A major milestone came in 2019 when Kamz Inkzone worked with Punjabi singer Ninja on “Befikra.”

The track became a notable hit associated with rap music and gave Kamz Inkzone greater visibility within the Punjabi music industry.

The project also helped establish his ability to work on music involving established artists.

Developing New Artists

Following the success of his projects, Kamz Inkzone began receiving opportunities to work with different singers and emerging artists.

He became involved in creating music and launching new talent, including Hunar Sidhu. The song “Sade Siro”became one of the major milestones of this phase, crossing 25 million views.

The success of the project brought additional attention to Kamz Inkzone's work behind the music.

Working With Bohemia

Kamz Inkzone's career later included a collaboration with Bohemia, one of the most recognised names associated with Punjabi rap.

For Kamz Inkzone, working alongside an artist of Bohemia's stature represented an important step in his evolution within the music industry.

International Projects in London, Dubai and Europe

His professional journey has also expanded internationally. Kamz Inkzone has travelled to London, Dubai and Europe for music shows, private performances and music video projects.

These international experiences have become another part of his career as he continues exploring different forms of music and entertainment.

Exploring DJ and Remix Music

After working extensively in Punjabi music, Kamz Inkzone expanded his skills into DJing. He learned DJ techniques and started creating music specifically for the DJ space.

In 2025, he began experimenting with Afro music remixes and Punjabi remixes, while simultaneously working on original songs and collaborations.

A Multi-Dimensional Music Creator

Today, Kamz Inkzone works across several areas of the music industry. His experience includes music production, songwriting, DJ music, remix creation, artist collaborations and music video production.

He has also worked as an artist with platforms and labels including Speed Records, Taj Music and Stylebase.

In 2026, he established his own production company, Inkboy Musik, further expanding his role within the industry.

With Inkboy Musik working across Qawwali, hip-hop, DJ remixes and commercial Punjabi music, Kamz Inkzone is continuing to build a broader catalogue while developing his own identity as a music creator.
